Original sources

Retrieved 2026-09-30 unless noted. Every value carries its own school year, because the sources publish at different times: CPS enrollment and demographics are SY2026-27, while the state's test, attendance, graduation and spending figures are SY2024-25.

20th-day membership report, "Schools by Grade" sheet

Publisher: Chicago Public Schools (CPS)

Where: Demographics page

Years we use: SY2024-25, SY2025-26, SY2026-27 (counts on the 20th school day, in September; the SY2026-27 file is dated 2026-09-21)

What we take from it: The school roster (School ID, name, network, governance, school type, community area) and enrollment by grade, from the columns School ID, School Name, Network, Governance, School Type, Community Area, Total and the grade columns PE to 12.

Original files we used:
SY2026-27 file
SY2025-26 file
SY2024-25 file

To get it yourself: Open the Demographics page and choose the 20th-day membership report for the year. The header row is the second row of the sheet, and a "District Total" row has to be dropped.

20th-day English learner, IEP and low-income report, "Schools" sheet

Publisher: Chicago Public Schools (CPS)

Where: Demographics page

Years we use: SY2024-25, SY2025-26, SY2026-27

What we take from it: Share of students who are English learners ("State English Learners"), have an IEP ("Students with IEPs", called "Students with Disabilities" before SY2026-27) and are low income ("Low Income", called "Economically Disadvantaged" before SY2026-27). CPS publishes shares as fractions between 0 and 1; we show them as percentages.

Original files we used:
SY2026-27 file
SY2025-26 file
SY2024-25 file

To get it yourself: Same page as above; choose the "LEP, IEP, low income" report for the year.

FY2027 budget, Interactive Reports (line-item data export)

Publisher: Chicago Public Schools (CPS)

Where: FY2027 budget page

Years we use: FY2027 proposed budget; FY2026 adopted budget and projected spending (as of July 2026)

What we take from it: School budget dollars and positions. The export has one row per budget unit, fund, account and program, with the columns Unit, Unit Name, Fund Grant, Account, Program, FY26 Adopted Budget, FY26 Ending Budget, FY26 Projected Expenditures, FY27 Proposed Budget and the position counts. We add up the rows of each school's unit ("U" followed by the school's finance ID). The file's total ($10.11 billion) matches CPS's published FY27 budget; school units are $5.38 billion of it, and the rest is district-wide spending such as pensions, debt and central offices.

To get it yourself: On the FY2027 budget page, open "Interactive Reports" and use the report's Download Data option to export the line items as CSV. The page also has public Excel files ("FY27 School Budget Overview") with staffing counts for each school but no dollar totals for district-run schools. We refresh this export by hand once a year.

Illinois Report Card Public Data Set (2023, 2024 and 2025 editions)

Publisher: Illinois State Board of Education (ISBE)

Where: Report Card data page

Years we use: 2025 edition = SY2024-25; 2024 = SY2023-24; 2023 = SY2022-23

What we take from it: Test results, attendance, chronic absenteeism, graduation, freshman on-track, college enrollment, 5Essentials survey levels, per-pupil spending and the share of students in temporary living situations or in care. We read the sheets General, IAR, ACT, SAT and Finance, and keep the rows where Level is School and the school ID begins 15-016-2990 (Chicago Public School District 299). A "*" in the data means ISBE hid a small group; we show it as suppressed, never as zero.

Original files we used:
2025 edition (xlsx, 40 MB)
2024 edition (xlsx, 55 MB)
2023 edition (xlsx, 24 MB)

To get it yourself: Download the workbook from the Report Card data page. Read columns by their header names, not their position. ISBE revises the files after release (the workbook has a Revision History sheet); we used the revision current on 2026-09-30.

Chicago Board of Education subdistrict map (shapefile ERSB_20_Sub_District_Map_FA1_SB_15)

Publisher: Illinois Senate Democratic Caucus redistricting site

Where: Shapefile (zip)
Board of Elections map (PDF)

Years we use: The 20 subdistricts (1a to 10b) enacted by Public Act 103-0584

What we take from it: The subdistrict outlines on the map, and each school's subdistrict, found by placing its coordinates inside the polygons. The Chicago Board of Elections publishes the official map only as PDF pictures, so we compared the shapefile with it side by side.

To get it yourself: Use your own address at chicagoelections.gov to confirm your subdistrict.

ACT/SAT Concordance Tables (2018) and ACT College Readiness Benchmarks

Publisher: ACT and College Board

Where: Concordance tables (PDF)

Years we use: Fixed tables

What we take from it: Used only for the optional "score vs ACT benchmark" estimate for high schools: SAT school averages (SY2022-23 and SY2023-24) are converted to the ACT scale with the concordance table, then compared with the ACT national benchmark (ELA 20, math 22).

To get it yourself: Both are published on act.org.

Reading the files

schools.csv has one row per school with the latest value of each measure and its year. school_values.csv has one row per school, measure and year (school_id, metric, school_year, value, status, unit, source, retrieved). status is value or suppressed (the state hid a small group). A suppressed row has a blank value. If a school has no row for a measure and year, the source has no data for it. 'In schools.csv both cases are blank, so use school_values.csv to tell them apart. Neither is zero. school_id is the CPS School ID, and rcdts is the state's school ID.

Before comparing across years, read the methodology and caveats: the 2025 state tests are not comparable with earlier years, and test scores and spending are about a year older than enrollment.

The code that builds these files is MIT licensed and public: github.com/cmdshftateya/cps-facts.
