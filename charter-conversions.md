## Verified findings

**Acero.** Acero announced in Oct 2024 that it would close about half its schools because of a roughly $40M deficit. On Feb 27, 2025 the Board voted 16-3 to save five of the seven campuses slated for closure. Paz and Cruz closed in June 2025. CPS funded the five remaining campuses (Cisneros, De Las Casas, Fuentes, Tamayo, Santiago) through SY2025-26, and the Board directed the district to transition them to district-run schools by 2026-27. An earlier Dec 2024 board action had set this direction.
- CPS calls them "District-managed" schools that "join the CPS family." The cps.edu transition pages say they stay in their current buildings, with current students continuing. This is continuity language, but the pages do not literally say "continuation."
- Leadership changed. CPS named new principals, for example Bailenson at Fuentes, Munoz at Tamayo and Kornblau at Casas.
- Staff continuity is not guaranteed. CTU said CPS refused job guarantees for existing staff.
- Sources:
  - https://www.chalkbeat.org/chicago/2025/02/28/school-board-votes-to-save-5-acero-charter-schools/
  - https://chicago.suntimes.com/education/2024/12/20/7-acero-charter-schools-to-be-spared-from-closure-this-year-cps-plans-to-absorb-5-in-2026
  - https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/chicago-board-of-education-votes-to-keep-acero-schools-open
  - https://www.cps.edu/campaigns/acero-transitions/

**ChiArts.**
- The Board voted unanimously on Nov 5, 2025 to convert ChiArts from a charter into "a District-managed fine and performing arts high school" effective July 1, 2026.
- The Board's own statement says it will "preserve the conservatory model," which is continuity language. A City Bureau roundup (Sep 2026) says it transitioned to full CPS management and keeps a partnership with the Puerto Rican Arts Alliance.
- Caveat: the CPS and Board wording is "winding down its current form," so the charter entity ended and a new district school was established. Whether staff and students carry over one-to-one is not confirmed by the pages I read.
- A funding dispute over an extra $5.5M was postponed in Jul-Aug 2026 (Chalkbeat 2026/08/27, WBEZ 2026/07/30), which I did not read in full.

**EPIC.** The Board approved $1.4M on Nov 5, 2025 to keep it open through June 2026, after which it closes (8255 S. Houston Ave). The board statement says the district will develop a transition plan for students. It is a closure, not a conversion.

**ASPIRA (both high schools).** After months of cash advances (about $2.5M), CPS began letting students transfer in late Feb 2026. ASPIRA said it would close both schools by Apr 3, 2026. The Board unanimously revoked the charter on Mar 19, 2026.
- About 500 to 700 students moved. Chalkbeat says about 75% went to district schools, with the largest group (235) at Carl Schurz HS. City Bureau says nearly 700 were relocated; the figures differ by source.
- ASPIRA's CEO said he planned legal action; I did not check the outcome.
- These are closures with no district successor.

## Unconfirmed or conflicting

**Urban Prep Bronzeville (400105).** Sources conflict.
- A Chalkbeat article, May 29, 2026 (https://www.chalkbeat.org/chicago/2026/05/29/chicago-public-schools-tables-charter-school-renewals/), says the Board amended the Urban Prep Academy for Young Men contract "to wind down its Bronzeville campus and consolidate operations into its Englewood campus." I read this only through a fetch summary.
- Other results say both campuses stay open through at least SY2026-27 (the WTTW May 2025 renewal piece, https://news.wttw.com/2025/05/29/cps-renews-urban-prep-charter-schools-contract-after-district-previously-sought-take).
- The City Bureau Sep 2, 2026 summary says only "Renewed through 2026-27; district will operate Bronzeville and Englewood campuses." That wording is odd and I could not verify it.
- I could not find the effective date of any wind-down or whether it is a merger into Englewood. The 20th-day absence of 400105 is consistent with a consolidation into Englewood, but that is not confirmed. Check the May 2026 board report or minutes at cpsboe.org.

**Virtual Academy HS/ES (610597/610596).** I found no reporting or CPS statement about these being closed, merged, renamed or restructured for 2026-27. The cps.edu pages (https://www.cps.edu/schools/virtual-academy/ and https://www.cps.edu/schools/profiles/school-overview/virtual-academy-es) exist, but I did not confirm their SY26-27 status. They are district-run medical-need schools (about 500 students). The absence may be a data-file artifact (an ID change, or a program recoded under another school). Treat as unknown. Ask CPS or check the school-ID reference file.

## Other caveats
- None of the sources I read publish CPS school IDs for the new schools. The Acero and ChiArts pairings rest on name matches, which are strong but unverified against an official ID crosswalk.
- The Casas, Cisneros and Tamayo pages were seen only via search-result summaries. Fuentes, the general Acero page, the ChiArts page and the Santiago lease article were fetched directly.
- A 2026 Chalkbeat article says the Board renewed six charter networks on Jun 10, 2026. I did not confirm which networks were renewed.
